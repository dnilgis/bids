GFE Board of Governors Nominee Questionnaire

Granite Falls Energy, LLC (the “Company”) is preparing for its March 2027
annual members meeting and the Governor elections that will take place at that meeting.

The purpose of the questionnaire is to obtain information from potential
Governor nominees so the Company’s nominating committee is able to
recommend nominees for election to the Board.
