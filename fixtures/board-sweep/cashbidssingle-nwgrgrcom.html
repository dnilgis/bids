Daily Morning Commentary

Published on: Sep 25, 2026


Friday, September 25th, 2026


Northwest Grain Growers invites members to join us for an educational speaker series focused on timely issues impacting your farm, your cooperative, and the soft white wheat market. Each session will feature practical information from industry experts on topics such as crop insurance, wheat quality, farm programs, commodity markets, grain marketing, transportation, and regional river-system issues. The first session will feature speakers Gary Heilig Jr. on crop insurance, Brian Liedl on white wheat quality issues, and Riley Kretschmer on grain marketing. Check your NWGG email newsletter for more information about the speakers and topics.

When: Monday, September 28, 2026, beginning at 10:00 a.m.

Where: Walla Walla Fairgrounds Community Building

Details: Lunch will be provided. Please RSVP by emailing office@nwgrgr.com
