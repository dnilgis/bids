Daily Commentary 9-25

Market News:

President Xi and Trump met yesterday. It was expected that headlines would come from this meeting, however all that has been said is they reached a common understanding on many issues. 

Talks to reopen the Black Sea seem to be gaining momentum this week. Zelenskiy said on Friday that India, Turkey, Egypt and other middle eastern nations are participating in discussions to unblock the shipping route. 

The U.S. and Iran are considering a phased agreement that would reopen the Strait of Hormuz. 

Weather:

It is likely to get rain around 10 this morning. Afterwards strong winds will persist through most of the afternoon. 

Frost and hail hit Argentina’s wheat crop, plummeting their G/E ratings to 56%. 

Futures, Basis and Cash:

Funds seem to be liquidating longs in all market after the meeting with Xi and Trump.

Grains markets are significantly weaker this morning. Chicago wheat is down 15, KC wheat is down 14, Corn is down 5 and soybeans are down 10.


Grain Marketing Hours: Monday- Friday 8:30AM-11:10AM. Monday-Thursday 2:30-4:30PM
