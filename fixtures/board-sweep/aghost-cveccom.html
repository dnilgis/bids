Closed Monday 9/7 in Observance of Labor Day

Due to bin maintenance and lost storage space, CVEC will be contract corn only through FH Sept. We are still currently accepting new LH Sept or later delivered contracts.

OFFICE HOURS: 8:00am to 4:30pm

DDGS Loading Hours

Monday - Thursday:  6:00am to 3:30pm

Friday:  6:00am to 2:30pm

Attn: CVEC Corn Suppliers

CVEC was recently issued an Air Permit renewal by the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency (MPCA). Written into the permit is a restriction that limits our daily unload of corn delivered via end-dump trailers to 2,500 bushels. According to the MPCA, this restriction is due to additional dust emissions by end-dump trailers versus hopper-bottom trailers. We realize this is ridiculous and apologize for the inconvenience that this will cause several of our corn customers. We are working towards a solution, but in order to stay in compliance with MN state regulations we will be forced to monitor and adhere to this limit in the interim.

 

Thank you for your understanding and please feel free to call Brody or Sara with any questions.

To contract corn or to place offers, contact Brody or Sara at 320-843-1231.
