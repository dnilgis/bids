Every year, we produce 30 million gallons of ethanol.

We purchase 10 million bushels of corn every year from over 500 local family farms in West Central Minnesota. The resulting dried distiller grains (DDGS) and Wetcake – 52,000 tons and 25,000 tons respectively – return to farms and feed their livestock.
