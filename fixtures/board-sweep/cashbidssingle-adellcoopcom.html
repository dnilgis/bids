45Z and Low Carbon Corn
Still thinking about enrolling your 2025 corn bushels in our low-carbon corn program? Don’t wait too long—our program is already 55% full for 2025 bushels enrolled through Continuum Ag.
If you delivered and sold corn from your 2025 crop to Adell Cooperative, you may be eligible to participate. With program space filling up, now is the time to get started.

Corn Silage Dry Down Days
Fall is right around the corner, and silage harvest will be here before we know it. Check out our upcoming Silage Dry Down Days—we have several opportunities available to accommodate your harvest schedule.
This year, Adell Cooperative is also partnering with UW Extension to collect data from this year’s corn silage crop. When bringing in your samples, please be prepared to provide the town of the field, brand, hybrid, relative maturity, planting date, BMR variety, fungicide use, and previous crop planted.
The information collected will help provide a better picture of this year’s silage crop across the area.
Thank you for your continued patronage, and here’s to a safe and successful silage harvest!

Credit Policy

  • A finance charge of 1.5% (an annual percentage rate of 18%) will be applied to any balance resulting from purchases made during a calendar month that is not paid before the last day of the following month, as well as any previous unpaid balance.
  • Payments will be allocated to the oldest outstanding balance.
  • If we must initiate collection proceedings to recover any outstanding balance, patrons may be liable for statutory court costs and attorney fees.
  • Adell Cooperative reserves the right to modify the terms and conditions of the Credit Policy, notifying patrons as required by law.
  • For joint accounts, both parties agree to abide by the terms of the Credit Policy and accept joint and several liability for payment of all purchases made under the Credit Policy.
  • Adell Cooperative has the right to limit or terminate a patron’s charge account, but this will not affect the obligation to pay any existing balance.
  • Adell Cooperative may issue a written default notice and may choose to declare the entire balance due and payable.
  • In accordance with its Articles of Incorporation and By-Laws, Adell Cooperative holds a first lien security interest on the capital stock or equities of the Adell Cooperative held by any patron for any debt owed by the patron.
