# data/gaps

Worklists about OUR coverage and OUR manifests. None of them is a claim about
anyone's business.

Every file here is REGENERATED WHOLESALE, which is why .gitattributes declares
data/gaps/* as merge=generated. The two below come from scripts/gap_lists.mjs;
the rest name their own generator in their section.

## no-website-on-file.csv
Businesses we know of with no URL in our data. Every state registry entry is
here, because states publish LICENSEES, not websites — so ADM, Bunge, Cargill,
CHS, MFA and Landus are all in this file. They plainly have websites. Nobody has
looked one up yet. The rows carrying a phone are the callable list.

## board-not-read.csv
Sites we have, loaded in a browser, and came away from without a board. As of
the first hand audit of twelve rows: one genuine negative, one behind a customer
login, and nine that publish a board this sweep could not read. Treat every row
as UNRESOLVED until somebody opens it.

Two mechanical reasons a row lands here and means nothing about the operator:
  * the price rows are injected by JavaScript into a server-rendered skeleton;
  * the data path is disallowed by the site's robots.txt — AgriCharts sites put
    the real table under /markets/*.php, which is disallowed, and we do not
    fetch what a site tells crawlers not to.

Generated by scripts/gap_lists.mjs. Do not edit by hand.

## rounding-disagreement.csv
Locations of ONE operator, reading ONE board on ONE platform, that declare
DIFFERENT `cashRounding`. Six operator groups, 70 sources on 2026-09-08.

Not a defect list. A co-op can genuinely run two site configurations, and a board
rounds only on the days its futures quote lands on a fraction of a cent —
Riceland's Weiner published exact rows on 2026-09-04 and needed `round-cent` by
2026-09-08. A row here means "these two ought to agree and nobody has checked".

The rows that ARE wrong are caught by test/declared-rounding.test.mjs, which fails
when a manifest declares a mode the source's own committed capture refutes. That
found nine on 2026-09-08, eight of them claiming `floor-cent` — the claim that a
residual is never negative — on boards carrying −0.25c rows. Nothing was red,
because a minority of failing rows publishes as `lagging`.

`this_capture_supports` is empty when the source's own rows do not settle the
question by the margin lib/rounding.mjs requires before a mode may be stated.
Generated by scripts/rounding_audit.mjs --write. Do not edit by hand.
