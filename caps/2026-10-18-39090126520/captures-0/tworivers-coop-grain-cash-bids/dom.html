How to add Two Rivers Cooperatives Cash Bids page as an icon on your device:

 Apple iPhone or iPad: 

  • Open the Safari or Internet app.
  • Type “TwoRivers.Coop” in the search box and select “Go”
  • On TRC’s home page, scroll down to the “Cash Bids” button and click on it.    This will open the Cash Bids page.
  • Tap on the “Share” icon, a square with an arrow pointing upwards, at the bottom of the page.
  • Select “Add to Home Screen.”  You may need to scroll up to view all options. 
  • The link button will be named “Cash Bids – Two Rivers Cooperative,” but you can edit the name.
  • Tap “Add” in the upper right-hand corner.
  • Now, a button with the TRC logo will appear on your home screen linking to the Cash Bids page.

If you need further instruction, you can watch this short video.

 Android:

  • Open the Chrome or Internet app.
  • Type “TwoRivers.Coop” in the search box and select “Go”
  • On TRC’s home page, scroll down to the “Cash Bids” button and click on it.    This will open the Cash Bids page.
  • Tap the menu icon (3 dots in upper right-hand corner) and tap “Add to Home Screen.”
  • The name field is editable, so you can change the name you want to be associated with this website. 
  • Tap “Add” to complete the process.
  • Now, a button with the TRC logo will appear on your home screen linking to the Cash Bids page.

If you need further instruction, you can watch this short video.
