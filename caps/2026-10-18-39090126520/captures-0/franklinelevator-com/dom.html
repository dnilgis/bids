Welcome to Franklin Elevator, Inc.

Franklin Elevator Inc. was established on August 27, 1957 by Leo Edward Bergschneider. It has been a family owned and operated business for over 60 years. Our main facility is in Franklin with additional locations in Orleans since 1992 and Scottville beginning in the fall of 2017. Our main business is buying and selling of corn, soybeans and wheat as well as a dealership for Kent Feeds. We offer grain bids delivered to any of the three locations as well as bids for grain picked up on your farm. We offer storage and marketing for the three commodities as well as delivered bids to sourounding terminals in the area. 

We stock some bagged Kent Feed for livestock and pets and can get any other feed as needed by our customers. 

If we can help in any way please contact us at Franklin @ 217-675-2393 or Orleans and Scottville @ 217-478-2000
