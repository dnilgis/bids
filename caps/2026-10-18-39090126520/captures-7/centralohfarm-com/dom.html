THANK YOU! A big thank you to everyone who joined us in Green Camp for our Propane Customer Appreciation event. We truly appreciate your continued support and the opportunity to serve you.

Don't Forget About Our Summer Fill Program! Our Summer Fill Program is now underway and is available to all Central Ohio Farmers' Cooperative propane customers. It's the perfect time to top off your propane tank and take advantage of our seasonal pricing.

- Available for customer-owned propane tanks.

- Will Call customers: Please take a moment to check your tank level and give us a call if you'd like to participate.

- To qualify for the Summer Fill price, propane tanks must be at 60% or less.

If you have any questions or would like to schedule your fill, we're happy to help. Thank you for choosing Central Ohio Farmers' Cooperative. We value your business and look forward to serving you all year long.

Propane is for Everyone.

 

COFC’s corporate grain department is located at our Prospect location.  The grain department’s direct phone number is 740-382-8132. If you want to talk to someone in person, please visit our Prospect office at 1007 E. Water Street. For payments, you have the options of having your funds ACH’ed or mailed to you, or you can stop by Prospect.
