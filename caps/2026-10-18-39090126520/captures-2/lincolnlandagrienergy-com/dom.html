DP Programs

 

Corn delivered beginning Monday July 20, 2026 is eligible to be enrolled in Lincolnland Agri-Energy LLC Deferred Pricing (DP) Program while space permits.  This program is separate from any prior or future DP Programs and is reserved for new deliveries.  There will be a $.05 per bushel per month charge for the duration of the program. Bushels must be priced by Wednesday, June 30, 2027, and at that time, un-priced bushels will be priced at Lincolnland Agri-Energy's spot price.  

 

Free DP (Closed)

 Corn delivered beginning Tuesday May 26, 2026, is eligible to be enrolled in a Limited Time Free Deferred Pricing (DP) Program while space permits. This program is separate from any prior or future DP Programs and is reserved for new deliveries.There will be no DP charges for the duration of the program. Bushels must be priced by Wednesday, June 30, 2027, and at that time, un-priced bushels will be priced at Lincolnland Agri-Energy's spot price.
