Announcement: Heartland Co-op Acquires Legacy Propane

We are thrilled to share that Heartland Co-op has successfully acquired the propane business from Legacy Propane in the Allison, Iowa region.

This acquisition marks a significant milestone in Heartland Co-op's dedication to expanding our energy presence.  We will strengthen our footprint in Butler County and nearby communities, all while maintaining the reliable service and local expertise that our customers have come to expect.

We eagerly anticipate the opportunity to serve our new customers in Allison.
