Please welcome Nick Rife, our new Agronomy Account Manager serving Southwest Kansas and Southeast Colorado.

 

Nick grew up in Springfield, Colorado, and spent his summers in Utah. After graduating from Vilas High School, he worked before beginning college at Oklahoma Panhandle State University (OPSU). While attending OPSU, he met his wife, Charley.

After college, Nick began his agricultural career with Skyland Grain in Walsh, Colorado. He gained experience in a variety of operational roles, from dumping trucks during harvest to delivering fertilizer for the agronomy division. He later joined Bartlett Grain Company in Walsh, eventually becoming manager of the Walsh and Pritchett locations. When Bartlett was sold to North Fork Grain in 2016, Nick remained with the company as Operations Manager and later became General Manager. After six busy years, he returned to Skyland Grain, where he served as Western Area Manager and eventually Area Agronomy Manager. Throughout his career, Nick has developed a broad and well-rounded skill set, with experience in human resources, information technology, safety, grain origination and merchandising, agronomy, energy sales, and more.

 

Nick and Charley have three boys: Townes, Waylon, and Statler. When he isn’t working, Nick enjoys caring for his cattle and attending his sons’ sporting events.
