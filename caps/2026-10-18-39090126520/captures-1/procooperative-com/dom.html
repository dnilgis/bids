    SPECIAL ANNOUNCEMENT:

    Pro Cooperative’s Rolfe Feed Mill will be closing effective July 29th, 2022 and transitioning current feed customers through the month of August.
     

    Due to uncontrollable circumstances, Pro Cooperative will be closing the Rolfe Feed Mill Effective July 29th, 2022.  With the short notice of our closure, Pro will continue to make feed for transitioning customers during the month of August or until inventory runs out at the Rolfe Feed Mill. Pro chose to close the Rolfe Feed Mill due to retirement of personnel, lack of personnel, and the lack of margin at the Rolfe Feed Mill. With the closing of the Rolfe Feed Mill, this will impact some dairy and beef customers in the area.  Please know we have bagged feed options available as an alternative.  If you would need to order some bagged feed, please contact our feed department.
