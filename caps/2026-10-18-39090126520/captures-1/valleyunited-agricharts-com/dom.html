Valley United Co-op is a newly merged farmer-owned co-op between Reynolds United Co-op and Halstad Elevator Co.  We have locations up and down the middle of the Red River Valley. We handle wheat, corn, and soybeans, (edible beans in Buxton only). We also offer a full line of agronomy services, including Crop Protection Products, Crop Nutrients, Seed, Custom Application, Crop Scouting, and Soil Sampling.

Thank you to our patrons for making this growth possible. We look forward to continued growth and to serving you, our valued patron, for many years to come.
