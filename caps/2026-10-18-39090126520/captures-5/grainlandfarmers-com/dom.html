Grainland Farmers is engaged in 45Z opportunities, working to create value for our farmers and strengthen our position in the emerging low-carbon grain market.

 

There has been a lot of discussion surrounding 45Z programs as we have moved into harvest over the past couple weeks. With multiple locations well positioned for potential 45Z opportunities, along with our access to rail, we believe Grainland Farmers will have more opportunities for participation in these programs.

We continue to do our due diligence, as we never want to overpromise and underdeliver. Please be patient and know that we are actively working to identify opportunities that can help add value to your farming operation.

There is absolutely no rush to commit to any program at this time. Opportunities for the 2026 crop will continue to evolve well beyond harvest.

If you have questions or would like additional information, please contact anyone at

Grainland Farmers.
