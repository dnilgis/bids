Iroquois Bio-Energy Company

A legacy of production excellence since 2007, pioneering carbon sequestration solutions in Indiana. 

🏭 Facility Legacy

  • Operating since 2007 with a legacy of production
  • 19 million bushels of corn processed annually

⛽ Ethanol Output

  • 60 million gallons of ethanol production capacity
  • Valuable agricultural co-products generated

🌍 Carbon Sequestration

  • On-site geologic formations identified for storage
  • Pioneering and advancing carbon sequestration solutions
