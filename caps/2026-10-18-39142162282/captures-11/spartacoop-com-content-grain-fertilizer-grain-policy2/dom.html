Storage charges on all grain will be $.05/month. A minimum of 3 months storage on all grain. Any grain sold prior to being in storage for 3 months will be charged the balance of the 3 month minimum. Grain stored over 12 months will be charged an additional $.05/month.

Load Out is $.10/bu. Grain contracted to be hauled by Sparta Co-op is $.05/bu. No charge on grain sold to Sparta Co-op.

Corn Discount Schedule

Test weight shrink factor:

54.0 & up – No discount
52.0 to 53.9 – 1.0%
50.0 to 51.9 – 1.4%
47.0 to 49.9 – 1.8%
45.0 to 46.9 – 2.5%
43.0 to 44.9 – 3.6%
41.0 to 42.9 – 4.8%
39.0 to 40.9 – 6.0%

 

Foreign Material:

Under 3.0% - No discount
3.1 to 4.0% - $.02 per percent
4.1 to 5.0% - $.03 per percent
5.1 to 6.0% - $.04 per percent
6.1 to 10.0% - $.05 per percent
10.0% & Above – Subject to rejection

 

Damage:

Under 5% - No discount
5.1 to 7.0% - $.01 per percent
7.1 to 20.0% - $.02 per percent
20.1% & Above - $.03 per percent

 

Heat Damage:

Under .2% - No discount
.3 to 2.0% - $.02 per percent
2.1% & Over – Subject to rejection

 

Sour - $.10/bu     Musty - $.10/bu     Infested - $.20/bu

 

Soybean Discount Schedule:

Soybeans dry at 13.0% moisture.

13.0 % and under – 1% handling shrink and $.075/bu in charge
13.1% and over – Shrink to 13%, 1% handling shrink and $.075/bu in charge.
 
No in charge or additional 1% shrink on grain sold to Sparta Co-op upon delivery.
 

Test weight shrink factor:

54.0 & up – No discount
52.0 to 53.9 – $.01 per pound
50.0 to 51.9 – $.02 per pound
48.0 to 49.9 – $.04 per pound

 

Foreign Material:

Any foreign material over 1% will be deducted from total weight.
Any load testing over 5% foreign material is subject to rejection.

 

Damage:

Under 2% - No discount
2.1 to 4.0% - $.015 per ½ percent
4.1 to 8.0% - $.03 per ½ percent
8.1% & Above – Subject to rejection

 

Splits:

Under 20% - No discount
20.1 to 30.0% - $.01 each 5%
30.1 to 40.0% - $.02 each 5%
Over 40.0% - Subject to rejection

 

Heat Damage:

Under .2% - No discount
.3 to 1.0% - $.02 per each .1 percent
1.1 to 1.5% – $.03 per each .1 percent
1.6% & Over – Subject to rejection

 

Sour - $.10/bu     Musty - $.10/bu     Infested - $.15/bu
