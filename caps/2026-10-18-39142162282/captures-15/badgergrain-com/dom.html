Grain marketing options

Cash or spot price

Haul it in and take the spot price, which is determined at the close of market. Payment will be made within 7 days of delivery.

Forward contract

Lock in a price now for grain you deliver later. Minimum 5,000 bushels, in 5,000 bushel lots.

Storage

Store grain at 5 cents per bushel per month, and sell when you decide the price is right. A 10 cent per bushel load out fee will be charged if corn is pulled back out of storage.

Corn drying and shrink

Shrink: 1.4% per point of moisture above 14%.
