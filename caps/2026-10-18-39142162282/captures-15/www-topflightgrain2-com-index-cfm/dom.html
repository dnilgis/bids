Unapproved Traits
**NOTICE**

Roundup Ready 2 Xtend Soybeans:

Topflight Grain will accept Extend soybeans that have been grown using approved labeled herbicides.
 This is subject to change with approval of all traits. If you have any questions, please contact the Monticello office.



Topflight Grain will not accept any commodities with traits that are not approved in all markets. All loads may be subject to testing at any time without prior notice.
