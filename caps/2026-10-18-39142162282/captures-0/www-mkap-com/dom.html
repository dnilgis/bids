Welcome

Minn-Kota Ag Products is a Grain Marketing and Production Agriculture Supply Company. We are located in the Southern Red River Valley of Western Minnesota and Eastern North Dakota.

Minn-Kota Ag Products helps area farmers with all of their Chemical, Fertilizer, Seed, and Grain Elevator needs.
