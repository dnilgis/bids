Brennco, Inc.

A family owned and operated swine operation located in Allen County Ohio. Brothers, Stan and Kim, started out with a small operation, buying feeder hogs from local growers and selling these to market. In 1994 they decided to incorporate and start expanding the operation. Today, Brennco, Inc., has increased in size to include 25 contract wean to finish growers and 50 employees.
