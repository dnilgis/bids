Our services include:

  • Grain Purchasing & Marketing

BEYOND GRAIN STORAGE

Ethanol Production with CORN, LP

In 2004, Gold-Eagle diversified our grain handling to go beyond storage by creating the foundation of an ethanol plant, CORN, LP. Ethanol production began in 2005 and continues to be an asset to our business and the community, consuming around 60,000 bushels of corn per day and employing 40 full-time team members.
