Five Star CooperativeAgricultural Cooperative

Yielding Success for Over 100 Years

We are a farmer-owned agricultural supply cooperative based in New Hampton, Iowa that proudly serves rural communities across Northern Iowa and Southern Minnesota. As a full-service cooperative, we support individual farmers with farms of all sizes through our quality products, expert service, and commitment to long-term success.

At Five Star Cooperative, standing together means looking out for one another—on the farm and beyond. Through every challenge and every season, we grow stronger together.

Cooperative Member Benefits

Why Join the Cooperative?

As a member of Five Star Cooperative, you’re part of a trusted ag coop with deep roots and shared rewards. Our members gain access to:

Increased buying power and lower input costs
Bulk purchasing and shared services
Competitive financing through RateStar and expert support
Patronage dividends and shared profits
Technical help in agronomy, energy, feed, and grain
Representation on our board of directors
A customized business dashboard through Five Star Link
When we work together, we grow together. That’s the cooperative advantage.

Ag Input Financing

Smart financing, local support.

We understand the financial needs of farmers. With in-house financing through RateStar, you get low-interest loans for seed, fertilizer, fuel, LP, and other crop inputs. Enjoy easy applications, payment flexibility, and savings compared to traditional lenders built specifically for agricultural production.
