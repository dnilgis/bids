DISCOUNT SCHEDULE – CORN

All discounts are subject to change without notification.

Effective 9/15/26

DISCOUNT SCHEDULE – SOYBEANS

All discounts are subject to change without notification.

Effective 9/15/26

RATES FOR 2026 HARVEST

CORN DP:  .23 cents/bu. Fee to January 15, 2027, then 4.0 cents/bu. per month, prorated daily.

SOYBEANS DP: .27 cents/bu. Fee to January 15, 2027, then 5.0 cents/bu. per month, prorated daily.

DRYING RATES:

15.1 TO 17.0 = 2.5 CENTS PER ½ POINT

17.1 TO 30.0 = 2.0 CENTS PER ½ POINT

Above 30% moisture will be discounted at 5 cents per ½ point and subject to rejection.

SHRINK

CORN   DP:  Shrunk to 14% moisture

CORN SPOT/CONTRACT:  Shrunk to 15% moisture

A shrink factor of 1.4 % per point will apply to Corn DP, Spot or Contracted.

SOYBEANS DP/SPOT/ CONTRACT: Shrunk to 13% moisture

A shrink factor of 1.5% per ½ point from 13.1%-15.0%

A shrink factor of 2.0% per ½ point above 15.1% and subject to rejection

  1. A new minimum rate will be charged on 9/15/26 for all old crop bushels currently on DP.
  2. All grain not immediately sold or applied to contract will be charged the minimum DP rates one week after delivery on a load-by-load basis.
  3. Grain on DP cannot be applied to sales contracts and must be sold at the nearby cash price.
  4. All rates and policies are subject to change without notification as dictated by market conditions.
  5. For a current discount schedule for both corn and beans please check our website.

All rates effective 9/15/26
