United Wisconsin Grain Producers (UWGP) is a farmer-owned ethanol production facility in Friesland, Wisconsin, focused on producing renewable fuel and high-quality co-products. By supporting local corn markets and delivering ethanol, distillers grains (DDGS), and yeast products, UWGP plays a key role in advancing sustainable agriculture and energy solutions across the Midwest.
