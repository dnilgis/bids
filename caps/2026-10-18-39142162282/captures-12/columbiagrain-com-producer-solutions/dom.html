Columbia Grain Producer Solutions

Columbia Grain Producer Solutions offers the up-to-date targeted marketing insights and education producers need to gain the competitive advantage. This innovative platform brings growth opportunities to our producers, highlighting better pricing tools to cultivate greater returns today and in the future. The platform fosters connectivity, reliability, and above all trusted relationships with each farmer, bringing them marketing products and risk management consulting that will address their specific challenges.

"Columbia Grain Producer Solutions gives our producers the market advantage," said Columbia Grain’s Director of Risk Management, Phil Symons. "Our tools are strategically selected to help increase their return. All tools are different, and market conditions vary. Our goal is to highlight the available tools and offer the guidance needed to help evaluate which tool fits best for each farmer."
