About us..................

The Farmers Elevator & Exchange Co. is a locally owned farm cooperative that serves the needs of its patrons in a four county area here in Northeast Missouri.  In addition to operating a country grain elevator that handles corn, soybeans and wheat, we are in the business to support animal nutrition and crop production!  We carry a full line of livestock and dairy feeds as well as feeds for the farm flock, pet, horse, goat, sheep and show feeds.  We are a dealer for Land O Lakes and Purina Feeds, as well as ADM Alliance Nutrition Feeds plus QLF liquid feeds.  Farmers Elevator & Exchange Co. offers grain bank services as well as feed processing (rolling & mixing); and bulk and bag feed delivery services.

 

     

Farmers Elevator & Exchange Co. has been helping farmers grow crops and produce grain and hay for a good long time.  In our geographic area, we provide quality crop input products and related services.  We are a dealer for both Golden Harvest and AgVenture brands of seed corn and soybean seed; AgriMaxx Seed Wheat; dry blend fertilizer; NH3; and crop protection products. We offer custom application of dry fertilizer and ag chemicals, Y-drop side-dress technology, grid soil sampling, mapping and spreading.  We have a shed full of custom application equipment all equipped with the latest technology of auto-steer/GPS guidance!

                 

This is your brand.

     

In the past century, the swine feeding business has moved far ahead of ‘slop buckets’ and handling 100 lb. sacks of tankage and grain milling byproducts for building swine rations.  Pitch forks and farrowing huts are not too common any longer either.  Genetics, nutrition and management are keys to success in the business of all livestock production.  Because of this, today’s growing and finishing hogs and breeding animals require quite different nutrients that just a few years ago to meet maximum production efficiency goals set by producers.  As a long-time supplier of animal nutrition, the Farmers Elevator & Exchange Company recently addressed these requirements and took things upon itself to create a feed milling facility whereas the company can produce feed precisely, effectively, safely, and efficiently.   We can now change rations to include the amount of grain, grain byproducts, macro and micro feed ingredients into feeding rations with a mouse and a keyboard and then manufacture products that promote animal growth and healthy animals for the producer.  In early 2017 we launched our Feed Mill UAE Plan that allowed us to incorporate an existing mill facility but to Update, Automate and Expand in order to accommodate the business we are now a part of.  We are very excited to bring this kind of a facility to the area and to support agriculture producers!
