Local Daily Commentary

Friday, OCT 16 2026 Morning Market Email: https://s3.amazonaws.com/media.agricharts.com/sites/2446/Markets_AM_10_16_26.pdf

 

Wheat futures are trading lower this morning, down 7 to 8 cents in the DEC futures. Corn, soybean, and wheat futures are trading lower this morning. Technical selling, fund liquidation, and ongoing harvest activity continue to weigh on prices. The Weekly Export Sales report came out this morning and was viewed as slightly supportive for corn, while soybean and wheat sales came in near the lower end of trade expectations. Traders remain focused on U.S. wheat harvest progress, South American planting and weather, and evolving geopolitical developments.

 

Managed to make it a whole morning without mentioning Russia and Ukraine or Iran....... Oops..........Since we mentioned it, President Trump called Ukraine's attacks on Russian oil infrastructure a "disgrace," and said Zelensky is "more difficult to deal with than Putin." Ukraine has seen limitations to its military-grade Starlink satellite system this week. When asked about the "mother of all bombs (MOAB)," Trump noted that it is not nuclear and that he won't use nuclear weapons and doesn't expect to use any more U.S. ground troops in Iran. Iran has reportedly been able to export 4.1 million barrels of LPG throught the U.S. blockade since July, but crude oil exports fell to zero in September.

 

Temperatures in the US Plains and Midwest are expected to see their first widespread frost tonight, with temperatures then forecast to moderate and remain warmer over the next couple of weeks. Harvest progress should make another notable jump in Monday's report, while winter wheat planting is likely to stay behind its average pace as wet conditions continue to slow fieldwork.

 

Cash Futures Prices: Chicago DEC is down 8 at 661. KC DEC is down 8 at 704 and MPLS DEC is down 7 at 678. US Dollar is trading 0.08 lower at 101.97.
