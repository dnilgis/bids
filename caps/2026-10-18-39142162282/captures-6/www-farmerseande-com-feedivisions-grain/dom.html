Farmers Elevator & Exchange has just over 4.6 million bushels of storage located a short distance from multiple grain terminals in Southeast Iowa including Grain Processing Corporation in Muscatine, IA; Bunge Grain at Meeker’s Landing; and Big River Resources West Burlington, LLC.

Customers have the advantages of grain storage, grain bank for feed corn, several contracting options, and custom drying.


Contracts & Programs Available:

  • Basis Contract
  • Cash Sales
  • Deferred Payment
  • Delayed Pricing (Price Later)
  • Direct Ship
  • Insight
  • Hedge-to-Arrive
  • Seasonal Mean Price

Services:

  • Trucking
  • Grain Vac Rental

For further information about Farmers Elevator & Exchange’s grain policies and services please contact our FEE Grain Marketing Team at 800-820-2723, 319-523-5351, or by email.
