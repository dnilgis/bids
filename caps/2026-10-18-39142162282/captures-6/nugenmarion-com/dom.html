Fall Delayed Pricing 2026

Effective 9/28/2026, Price Later Fees will be $.06/bu per month prorated from the date of delivery ($.002 per day). The Final price must be established by 10/22/2027 or will be priced at the market close on 10/22/2027. Price Later bushels must be priced at the current spot bid and may not be applied to future contracts. Title of grain passes to Nugen Energy LLC at the time of delivery for Delayed Price bushels.

Sellers must notify a grain buyer that you want your grain placed on the Price Later Program before delivery. Our scale operators will not be taking instructions to place grain on price later contracts. Program is subject to end at any time.

 

 

Please contact Jill 605-648-2104 and let her know how you would like you payment processed. Check requests will be processed Tuesdays and Thursdays for grain delivered prior to those days per usual.

Grain must be paid out or put on a deferred payment contract within 30 days of delivery per South Dakota regulations.

 

Grain delivered without a contract and contract overruns are spot priced at the closing bid on the day of delivery. Spot grain delivered on Saturdays, Sundays, or market holidays will be priced at the closing bid of the most recent market close prior to delivery.

Checks will be issued Tuesdays and Thursdays. If you would like to pick up a check, let Jill or a grain buyer know by 11:00 the day of. Checks will be ready for pickup at 3:00. Otherwise, checks will be mailed out.
