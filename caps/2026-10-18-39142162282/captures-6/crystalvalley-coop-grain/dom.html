Why Crystal Valley Grain?

Honored to be the region’s trusted cooperative, our grain division has the resources and expertise to support our customers.

  • Extensive storage & handling capacity with over 25 million bushels across seven locations

  • Local grain marketers living and working in your region, providing timely advice and personal service

  • Modern facilities with fast receiving and load out capabilities to fit your harvest schedule

Portal: How It Works

My Account (opens in new tab)

Manage your grain offers and access key account info anytime with the Crystal Valley App or web portal. Here’s how:

  • 1

    Log into “My Account” via the app or website.

  • 2

    Navigate using the tabs for tools and information, including:

    • Bids: View current cash bids, futures, and options
    • Scale Tickets: See real-time scale tickets and grading info.
    • Grain Summary: Track storage and open contracts, and view payout info.
    • Crop Insurance Reports: Print delivery reports by date or commodity.
    • Settlements: Access past settlements and full detail sheets.
    • My Offers: Enter and view working grain offers.
    • Purchase Contracts: Review, track, and e-sign grain contracts.
    • Contracts Paid: See payment status and discount details.
    • Grain Receiving Hours: Check updated dumping hours and contact info.
    • Webcams: Monitor line status at Hope, Madelia, and Jackson.

You can enter your own grain offers directly from your phone—anytime, anywhere.
