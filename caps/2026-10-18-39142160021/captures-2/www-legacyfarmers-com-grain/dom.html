GRAIN

Welcome to Legacy Farmers Cooperative, your trusted partner in Northwest Ohio's agricultural landscape. With over 100 years of dedicated service, we boast a network of 8 cutting-edge grain facilities, seamlessly integrating rail and country elevators to deliver unparalleled speed and efficiency.

Our seasoned and skilled grain team is the backbone of our commitment to continuous improvement, ensuring swift and secure operations. During harvest, our locations extend their hours to align with your needs, keeping your operations in motion.

At the heart of our mission is our unwavering dedication to you, our esteemed farmer-owners. We are invested in your success and the prosperity of the next generation. By collaborating with us, you gain access to a team of marketing and operations experts driven by the pursuit of your business goals. We prioritize building trust through friendly service, open communication, a diverse range of product offerings, and valuable education.

Our grain marketing team is devoted to elevating your journey, propelling you to the next level in the ever-evolving world of agriculture. Partner with Legacy Farmers Cooperative, where your success is our legacy.
