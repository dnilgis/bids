Every time on this page is absolute, deliberately. This is a static file, so any elapsed-time phrasing would be frozen at the moment it was baked and would go on reassuring you long after it stopped being true — on the one page you would open to find out whether the reader had died. Compare the deadline above against your own clock.

A price being old is normal; their board sits still most of the day and all weekend. Not having looked is the problem, which is why the two clocks are separate.
